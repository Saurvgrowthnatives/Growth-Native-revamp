# Growth Natives Website Redesign
## Product Requirements Document — PRD

**Project:** Growth Natives Corporate Website Redesign  
**Website:** https://growthnatives.com/  
**Project Type:** UX/UI Redesign + Front-End Experience  
**Primary Goal:** Transform the current Growth Natives website into a modern, premium, AI-native digital transformation and MarTech website with stronger UX, visual storytelling, 3D elements, and purposeful motion.

---

# 1. Company Positioning

Growth Natives should not be positioned visually as a traditional digital marketing agency.

The company should be presented as a:

**AI-Native Digital Transformation, MarTech & Growth Consulting Company**

The website should communicate expertise across areas such as:

- AI-powered digital transformation
- Marketing technology
- CRM and marketing automation
- Salesforce
- Revenue operations
- Growth strategy
- Data and analytics
- Digital experiences
- Web and application development
- UI/UX and creative services
- Technology consulting

The overall website experience should feel closer to a premium technology consultancy, AI company, or enterprise SaaS brand than a conventional marketing agency.

---

# 2. Project Background

The existing Growth Natives website needs to be redesigned to improve:

- Overall user experience
- Visual hierarchy
- Brand perception
- Service communication
- Content discoverability
- Modernity of the interface
- Visual storytelling
- Interaction quality
- Conversion opportunities

The redesign should retain Growth Natives' existing brand identity while significantly modernizing how the company is presented.

The new website should feel:

**Modern + Intelligent + Premium + Technical + Human + Experimental**

The experience can incorporate sophisticated 3D visuals and motion, but these should support the communication rather than becoming decorative distractions.

---

# 3. Primary Objective

Create a website that immediately communicates:

> Growth Natives combines AI, strategy, technology, data, and creativity to build scalable growth systems for businesses.

Within the first few seconds, a visitor should understand that Growth Natives is not simply a marketing agency.

It is a technology-led growth and digital transformation partner.

---

# 4. Redesign Goals

The new website should achieve the following goals.

### 4.1 Strengthen Brand Perception

Make Growth Natives appear:

- Enterprise-ready
- Technologically advanced
- AI-native
- Strategic
- Innovative
- Credible
- Premium
- Global

### 4.2 Improve UX

Users should be able to quickly understand:

- What Growth Natives does
- Who Growth Natives helps
- What capabilities the company offers
- What business problems it solves
- Why Growth Natives is different
- What results it has generated
- How to start a conversation

### 4.3 Improve Visual Storytelling

Replace overly text-heavy experiences with a combination of:

- Interactive diagrams
- UI visualizations
- 3D objects
- Motion graphics
- Data visualization
- Animated workflows
- Client success metrics
- Product-style storytelling

### 4.4 Increase Conversion

The site should naturally guide users toward actions such as:

- Talk to an expert
- Book a consultation
- Explore services
- Explore case studies
- View capabilities
- Contact Growth Natives

# 7. Brand Gradients

### Primary Linear Gradient

This gradient can be used selectively for:

- Key visual moments
- Interactive highlights
- Glows
- 3D materials
- Active states
- Motion trails
- Data visualization

---

# 8. Visual Design Direction

The overall design language should be:

- Minimal
- Modern
- Enterprise SaaS-inspired
- Premium
- High contrast
- Spacious
- Grid-driven
- Strong typography
- Motion-led
- Technologically sophisticated

Avoid:

- Excessive card grids
- Generic agency layouts
- Too many gradients
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Overly colorful UI
- Random floating objects
- Decorative animation without meaning
- Generic AI-generated illustrations
- Heavy visual clutter
- Large walls of text

Every section should have a clear visual purpose.

---

# 9. Design Philosophy

Follow this principle:

> Show systems, intelligence, transformation, and outcomes rather than simply describing services.

Whenever possible, turn abstract ideas into visual systems.

For example:

Instead of showing a static "Marketing Automation" icon, visualize a workflow connecting customer signals, CRM data, automation, and revenue.

Instead of simply listing AI capabilities, visualize an intelligent system processing information and producing business outcomes.

Instead of presenting case studies as basic cards, highlight measurable transformation.

---

The layout should combine:

### Left / Primary Content

- Short supporting statement
- Primary CTA
- Optional secondary CTA

Typography should dominate rather than competing with the graphic.

### Right / Background Experience

Create a premium interactive visual representing:

- AI

- Data

- Connected systems
- Transformation

Possible directions include:

- Intelligent 3D network
- Connected digital ecosystem
- Data flowing through business systems

- Abstract 3D system visualization

- Robots
- AI brains
- Generic glowing spheres
- Random floating cubes
- Stock photography

# 12. 3D Design Direction

3D should become part of the visual language but must remain purposeful.

Possible uses:

- Connected data systems
- Interactive technology architecture
- Growth pathways
- Animated pipelines
- Orbital elements
- Connected nodes
- Modular system blocks
- Fluid data movement
- Abstract digital environments

Use techniques such as:

- WebGL
- Three.js
- React Three Fiber
- GSAP
- Shader effects where appropriate

3D should never make the website difficult to navigate.

---

# 13. Motion Design

Animation should help users understand relationships and hierarchy.

Recommended motion:

- Subtle text reveals
- Scroll-linked transformations
- Smooth section transitions
- Animated diagrams
- Data flow
- Object depth transitions
- Magnetic hover states
- Smooth cursor interactions
- Card expansion
- Parallax depth
- SVG path animation
- Interactive 3D responses

Avoid animating every element.

Important content should remain readable without waiting for an animation.

---

# 14. Scroll Behaviour

Scrolling should feel smooth and cinematic but remain usable.

Potential patterns:

- Sticky storytelling
- Horizontal content transitions within selected sections
- Depth changes
- Object scaling
- Smooth visual morphing
- Content entering and exiting along motion paths
- Section-to-section transformations

Do not turn the entire site into an experimental scrolling demo.

Use advanced scrolling only for important storytelling sections.

---

Required components may include:

**Solutions**

**Insights**

**Let's Talk**

---

# 17. Typography

Typography should feel:

- Modern
- Clean
- Technical
- Highly readable
- Strong at large display sizes

Avoid excessive font styles.

Recommended structure:

- Strong display heading style
- Clean body typography
- Small technical labels / eyebrow text
- Clear visual hierarchy

Use responsive typography through `clamp()` wherever appropriate.

---

# 18. Layout

Recommended desktop content width:

Approximately **1200–1440px**

Use:

- Strong grid systems
- Generous spacing
- Large margins
- Intentional whitespace
- Asymmetric compositions where useful

Avoid repeatedly placing everything inside centered rectangular cards.

---

# 19. Dark and Light Usage

The website may alternate intelligently between dark and light sections.

### Dark sections

Use for:

- AI
- Technology
- 3D visualizations
- Transformation storytelling

### Light sections

Use for:

- Content-heavy sections
- Case studies
- Insights
- Trust information

Transitions between dark and light should feel intentional.

---

# 20. Responsive Behaviour

Design mobile from the beginning.

The experience must work across:

- Large desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile

Complex desktop 3D interactions should gracefully simplify on smaller screens.

Never force desktop motion patterns onto mobile.

---

# 21. Accessibility

Follow WCAG best practices.

Requirements include:

- Proper heading structure
- Keyboard accessibility
- Visible focus states
- Sufficient contrast
- Semantic markup
- ARIA attributes where required
- Reduced-motion support

Use:

`prefers-reduced-motion`

to disable heavy animation for users who request reduced motion.

---

# 22. Performance

Despite using sophisticated animation and 3D, performance must remain a priority.

Target:

- Fast initial load
- Good Core Web Vitals
- Smooth 60fps animations wherever possible
- Lazy-loaded media
- Optimized models
- Compressed textures
- Optimized WebP / AVIF imagery
- Minimal blocking JavaScript

Do not load large 3D experiences until required.

---

# 23. Recommended Technology Stack

Suggested implementation:

**Framework**

React / Next.js

**Styling**

Tailwind CSS

**Components**

shadcn/ui where appropriate

**Animation**

GSAP  
Framer Motion where suitable

**3D**

Three.js / React Three Fiber

**Icons**

Lucide or custom SVG

# 24. Interaction Principles

Every interaction must satisfy at least one of these purposes:

1. Explain something
2. Guide attention
3. Show a relationship
4. Provide feedback
5. Strengthen the narrative

If an animation does none of these, remove it.

# 30. What NOT to Build

Do not create:

- A generic agency template
- A page containing endless identical service cards
- Random 3D blobs
- Neon cyberpunk styling
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Huge amounts of floating UI
- Overly colorful backgrounds
- Every-section scroll animation
- Random AI imagery
- Extremely long homepage
- A design inspired too literally by one reference website

The final design must retain a distinct Growth Natives identity.

---

# 31. AI Agent Design Instructions

When designing or coding the website, behave like a:

**Senior Product Designer + Creative Developer + UX Strategist + Motion Designer**

Before creating each section:

1. Understand what the section needs to communicate.
2. Determine whether text or visualization communicates it better.
3. Establish clear visual hierarchy.
4. Avoid unnecessary elements.
5. Ensure the section connects visually with the preceding section.
6. Use animation only when it improves communication.
7. Maintain the Growth Natives brand palette.
8. Keep the experience premium and enterprise-focused.

Do not simply generate standard SaaS cards.

Think in terms of:

**Story → Interaction → Information → Outcome**

---

# 32. Success Criteria

The redesign will be considered successful when:

- Users can understand what Growth Natives does within seconds.
- Growth Natives appears more like an AI / technology transformation partner than a generic digital agency.
- Services are easier to understand.
- Case studies communicate results clearly.
- Navigation becomes easier.
- The brand feels significantly more premium.
- 3D and animation enhance rather than distract from UX.
- Desktop and mobile experiences both feel intentional.
- The site maintains strong performance despite advanced visuals.
- Calls to action are clear and strategically placed.

---

# 33. Final Creative Direction

The final experience should sit at the intersection of:

**Enterprise Consulting × AI Technology × SaaS Product Design × Creative Technology**

It should communicate Growth Natives as a company capable of connecting:

**Strategy → Data → Technology → AI → Experience → Revenue**

The website should feel forward-looking without becoming futuristic for the sake of appearance.

Every visual decision should reinforce the idea that Growth Natives creates intelligent, connected systems that enable business growth.
