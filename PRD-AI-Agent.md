**EXECUTIVE PRODUCT BRIEF**

# Growth Natives Website Redesign

This PRD defines the product, experience, visual, motion, 3D, responsive, performance and implementation requirements for the next-generation Growth Natives website. It is intentionally written so a design or engineering AI agent can execute the project without reinterpreting the core direction.

> **North Star**
> Turn the Growth Natives website into a living demonstration of the company itself: intelligent, connected, AI-native, premium, measurable and built for growth.

## 1. Executive Summary

The redesign should move Growth Natives away from a conventional service-agency presentation and toward an enterprise-grade digital experience that feels like an intelligent growth operating system. The site must make a broad service portfolio easier to understand, prove capability quickly, and use motion and 3D only where they improve storytelling.

| From | To |
| --- | --- |
| Service catalogue website | Connected growth-system narrative |
| Many sections with equal visual weight | A clear story with controlled moments of intensity |
| Generic agency cards and icons | Realistic system UI, workflows, proof and outcomes |
| Decorative animation | Purposeful motion that explains relationships and progression |
| "We do everything" impression | Clear outcomes, modular capabilities and proof |

### Primary goals

- Clarify what Growth Natives is and why it is different within the first 5-10 seconds.
- Reframe Growth Natives as an AI-native growth systems partner, not a collection of disconnected agency services.
- Make Growth Pods, AI capabilities, cross-functional services and measurable outcomes visually understandable.
- Create a premium, modern, spatial experience using depth, atmospheric gradients, selective 3D and disciplined motion.
- Improve conversion paths to Talk to an Expert / Book a Conversation without making the experience feel sales-heavy.
- Maintain strong performance, accessibility, responsiveness and SEO despite the richer interaction layer.
### Non-goals

- Do not turn the website into a 3D demo reel, game or experimental art site.
- Do not use motion on every object or animate purely for novelty.
- Do not copy Antimetal, Papermark, roommaster or any other reference layout one-to-one.
- Do not introduce unrelated neon palettes such as purple, magenta, orange or rainbow gradients.
- Do not sacrifice readability, conversion clarity, accessibility or mobile usability for visual effects.

# 6. Homepage Structure and Requirements

Target: approximately 9-10 major experiences. Avoid a homepage that feels like 15-20 disconnected modules. Each section must have one clear job and one dominant visual idea.

## 6.1. Immersive Hero

> **Purpose**
> Communicate the Growth Natives category and differentiator immediately while delivering the strongest visual moment on the page.

### Content hierarchy

- Eyebrow: AI-NATIVE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION.
- Primary message direction: "Build the systems behind intelligent growth." Final copy may be refined but should stay concise and outcome-led.
- Support copy must connect AI, data, technology, marketing and people into one operating system for growth.
- Primary CTA: Talk to an Expert. Secondary CTA: Explore Our System / See How We Work.
- Optional compact trust signal below CTAs, never a dense logo wall inside the hero.
### Visual / layout requirements

- Dark base using GN Black with blue radial atmosphere and a minimal grid.
- One meaningful 3D "Growth Core" or connected-system object. Do not use a random abstract sphere.
- Core nodes may represent AI, Data, CRM, Creative, Engineering, RevOps / Growth.
- Strong typography with generous line length control. Hero must not become text-heavy.
- Visual can sit center-right or center-stage depending on prototype quality; copy remains immediately readable.
### Motion / interaction

- Slow ambient node motion / signal movement only.
- Pointer movement may create subtle camera parallax on desktop; cap motion amplitude.
- Scroll should begin the transition from GN Black -> deep blue -> luminous blue -> light surface.
- 3D scene must not block scrolling, capture the pointer unnecessarily or create motion sickness.
### Responsive behavior

- Tablet: reduce 3D depth and node count; keep the system readable.
- Mobile: use a simplified 2.5D / pre-rendered / CSS fallback if WebGL quality or performance is weak.
- Hero headline remains above the fold with CTA visible without requiring interaction.
### Acceptance criteria

- A new visitor can state what GN does after viewing the hero for 5-10 seconds.
- Primary CTA is visually dominant but not oversized.
- No more than one major 3D object is visible.
- Hero reaches interactive state quickly and does not delay LCP.
## 6.2. Trust and Proof Strip

> **Purpose**
> Establish credibility immediately after the hero and reduce perceived risk.

### Content hierarchy

- Short "Trusted to power growth" style label.
- Curated client / partner logo rail using monochrome treatment.
- Three high-value proof metrics. Use approved current metrics only.
### Visual / layout requirements

- Bright white / very light surface after the atmospheric hero transition.
- Large whitespace. Metrics should feel editorial, not like generic statistic cards.
- Logo rail should be restrained and subordinate to the proof metrics.
### Motion / interaction

- Logo motion can be very slow or static; do not create a distracting ticker.
- Metrics may count up once when they enter the viewport; no looping.
### Responsive behavior

- Mobile may stack metrics 1-up or 2+1 with logo rail below.
- No horizontal overflow from logos.
### Acceptance criteria

- Proof is visible before the user reaches the third major section.
- Metrics are legible, source-approved and not presented without context.
## 6.3. The Growth Problem: Disconnected -> Connected

> **Purpose**
> Explain the core strategic problem GN solves before listing services.

### Content hierarchy

- Headline direction: Growth breaks when everything works in isolation.
- Support message explaining fragmented CRM, marketing, data, creative, sales and AI.
- Transition statement that introduces Growth Natives as the connective operating layer.
### Visual / layout requirements

- Begin with separated modules / nodes: CRM, Marketing, Data, Creative, Sales, AI.
- As the narrative progresses, the nodes converge into one connected GN system.
- Use a clean technical diagram language, not a sci-fi interface.
### Motion / interaction

- Scroll-driven progression may connect one or two relationships at a time.
- Do not animate every line simultaneously.
- Completion state must stay visible long enough to understand.
### Responsive behavior

- On mobile, convert to a vertical step sequence or simplified connection animation.
- No tiny labels that require zooming.
### Acceptance criteria

- The concept is understandable without reading every paragraph.
- The animation communicates a business idea, not only visual spectacle.
## 6.4. Growth Pods

> **Purpose**
> Productize the Growth Pods model as a flexible, connected team rather than another service list.

### Content hierarchy

- Headline direction: One team. Built around your growth problem.
- Short explanation of configurable cross-functional expertise.
- Representative roles can include Strategy, RevOps, Lifecycle Marketing, AI Engineering, Design, Data and platform specialists.
- CTA: Explore Growth Pods.
### Visual / layout requirements

- Use a large operating-canvas / workspace visual rather than six equal cards.
- Center state: YOUR GROWTH POD. Specialists are added around / into the pod.
- Final state should communicate a coordinated team, not freelancers floating independently.
### Motion / interaction

- On scroll, specialists join the pod in a controlled sequence.
- Subtle connecting lines / responsibility paths appear as the pod becomes active.
- Completion may show "POD READY" or a similarly concise status treatment.
### Responsive behavior

- Tablet: reduce roles shown at once.
- Mobile: use a swipeable or stepped pod-building interaction with no mandatory drag gestures.
### Acceptance criteria

- The section explains the model in under 20 seconds.
- No crowded orbit of small labels.
- CTA remains visible after the animated explanation.
## 6.5. AI-Native Operating System

> **Purpose**
> Show AI as an integrated operating capability, not a novelty feature or separate tool.

### Content hierarchy

- Headline direction: AI should not be another tool. It should become part of how you operate.
- Capability nodes may include Predict, Automate, Personalize, Qualify, Analyze and Optimize.
- Each node reveals a concrete business use case rather than generic AI language.
### Visual / layout requirements

- Central GN AI / intelligence node connected to capability clusters.
- Technical but calm visual language: data paths, nodes, state changes, live-like UI labels.
- Use blue as the dominant system color with selective green success states.
### Motion / interaction

- Hover / focus activates one capability at a time and expands supporting information.
- Scroll may introduce clusters, but direct exploration should remain possible after the reveal.
- Keyboard focus must provide the same informational state as hover.
### Responsive behavior

- Mobile uses a vertical accordion / carousel paired with a simplified system diagram.
- No hover-only critical information.
### Acceptance criteria

- Every AI capability shown maps to an understandable business outcome.
- The visual remains comprehensible even with animation disabled.
## 6.6. Connected Capabilities

> **Purpose**
> Communicate the breadth of GN without returning to a long service catalogue.

### Content hierarchy

- Section statement: Everything growth needs. Connected.
- Primary groups: Revenue, AI + Automation, Data, Digital Experience, Technology / Engineering.
- Each group receives one concise outcome line and a path to deeper detail.

- Use an asymmetric modular canvas / editorial bento, not six identical feature cards.

- Avoid stock illustrations and generic icons as the main visual.
### Motion / interaction

- Hover may introduce 2-4px perspective, highlight related nodes or reveal a short detail.
- No bouncing, floating loops or strong tilt effects.
### Responsive behavior

- Grid collapses to 1-column on mobile in an intentional order.
- Interactive content must remain tappable and fully visible.
### Acceptance criteria

- User can understand the capability model without opening the mega menu.
- Modules feel related through one system, not separate mini landing pages.
## 6.7. Results / Growth in Numbers

> **Purpose**
> Translate capability into measurable business value.

### Content hierarchy

- Headline direction: Systems are only useful when they move the numbers.
- Use 3-5 approved outcomes with clear labels and sources / case references where appropriate.
- Link metrics to relevant work or case studies.
### Visual / layout requirements

- Very clean white or near-white editorial section.
- Large numerals, minimal containers, strong whitespace.
- No decorative 3D required.
### Motion / interaction

- One-time count-up or subtle reveal only.
- Allow the calm section to contrast with earlier immersive content.
### Responsive behavior

- Numbers remain large and readable on small screens.
- Avoid animation-driven layout shifts.
### Acceptance criteria

- Every statistic includes enough context to be credible.
- No invented or placeholder results in production.
## 6.8. Featured Work / Case Studies

> **Purpose**
> Provide high-confidence proof through cinematic, measurable client stories.

### Content hierarchy

- Feature approximately 3 flagship cases on the homepage.
- Each case includes client, concise challenge / transformation, one dominant result and CTA.
- Use real interface / campaign / system imagery when approved.
### Visual / layout requirements

- Oversized horizontal / near-full-viewport panels instead of small three-column cards.
- Strong visual hierarchy: case number, client, one statement, one metric, large visual.
- Maintain brand restraint; client work should have room to breathe.
### Motion / interaction

- Scroll can pin / transition between panels only if it remains intuitive and short.
- Avoid excessive scroll-jacking. Users must retain standard page control.
- Case panels may reveal image depth or mask transitions subtly.
### Responsive behavior

- Mobile uses stacked cases with standard scrolling. Do not reproduce complex pinned desktop behavior if it harms usability.
### Acceptance criteria

- Case study title and outcome are understandable at a glance.
- Interactive behavior never hides the CTA or result.
## 6.9. Client Voices

> **Purpose**
> Add human proof and qualitative confidence without creating a wall of testimonial cards.

### Content hierarchy

- One featured testimonial in focus with name, role, company and relevant result if approved.
- A smaller rail / index of additional testimonials.
- Use authentic portraits or logos only.
### Visual / layout requirements

- Minimal white / quiet surface.
- Large quote with excellent line length. Supporting rail remains visually secondary.
### Motion / interaction

- Horizontal drag / click may switch the featured testimonial.
- Autoplay is not required and should not compete with reading.
### Responsive behavior

- Mobile switches to a simple swipeable carousel with clear controls.
- Quote length should not create excessively tall slides.
### Acceptance criteria

- Testimonials are readable without auto-rotation.
- All controls are keyboard accessible.
## 6.10. Final Immersive CTA

> **Purpose**
> Close the narrative by returning to the connected system and making the next action obvious.

### Content hierarchy

- Headline direction: Ready to build what is next?
- One short support line.
- Primary CTA: Talk to an Expert.
- Optional secondary link: View Our Work / Explore Growth Pods.
### Visual / layout requirements

- Transition white -> pale blue -> electric blue -> deep navy / GN Black.
- Return of the Growth Core or connected system, now in a completed / stable state.
- Footer remains clean and legible, not buried under the 3D scene.
### Motion / interaction

- Ambient completion state only; avoid an entirely new animation concept.
- CTA should not move or dodge the pointer.
### Responsive behavior

- Mobile keeps the CTA and footer content ahead of decorative visual complexity.
### Acceptance criteria

- The page ends with a clear action, not an ambiguous animation.
- Footer links are fully accessible and legible.
**MOTION AND SPATIAL DESIGN**

# 7. Motion and 3D System

## 7.1 Motion hierarchy

| Layer | Intensity | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero / signature scene | High | One primary spatial story, ambient motion, meaningful interaction. |
| Major explanatory sections | Medium | Scroll reveals, system connections, progressive assembly. |
| Supporting sections | Low | Opacity, translate, blur, subtle scale, restrained hover states. |
| Proof / reading sections | Very low | Mostly static; focus on content and trust. |

## 7.2 Preferred motion language

- Use transforms, opacity, blur and subtle perspective instead of constant looping motion.
- Typical reveal: translateY(24-40px) -> 0, opacity 0 -> 1, blur 6-10px -> 0.
- UI surfaces may settle from scale 0.96 -> 1 and rotateX 3-5deg -> 0.
- Use stagger sparingly and keep reading order obvious.
- Use the same easing family across the site. Avoid combining many animation personalities.
- Scroll-linked scenes must complete predictably and never require pixel-perfect scrolling.
## 7.3 3D implementation rules

- Limit production Three.js / React Three Fiber experiences to 1-2 signature areas unless performance testing proves more is safe.
- Use one shared render loop per scene and pause / reduce rendering when offscreen.
- Prefer instancing and lightweight geometry. Compress textures and avoid unnecessary high-poly assets.
- Lazy-load WebGL after critical text / layout. 3D must not be the LCP element unless carefully optimized.
- Provide a high-quality static / CSS / video fallback for unsupported or low-power devices.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion and allow a visually complete non-animated state.
## 7.4 Cursor and microinteraction

- Optional enhanced cursor on desktop only, limited to contexts such as View Case, Drag or Explore.
- Never replace the native pointer in forms, navigation, links or standard controls.
- Buttons use small responsive movement / highlight, not magnetic movement so strong that targets shift away.

## 8.1 Dark / light rhythm

The homepage should breathe through controlled surface transitions, not alternate colors every section. Recommended macro rhythm:

> **Surface sequence**
> GN Black hero -> blue atmospheric transition -> white proof/story -> selected blue-tinted system section -> white results / work -> blue-to-dark final CTA.

## 8.2 Avoid list

- Random purple / magenta / orange gradients.
- Generic AI brains, robots, glowing heads or abstract neural blobs as primary visuals.
- Excessive glassmorphism or transparent cards over noisy backgrounds.
- Constant floating, bouncing or orbiting UI with no explanatory purpose.
- Large walls of text inside cards.
- Tiny light-grey text used as a visual style at the expense of readability.
- Visibly AI-generated human portraits.

## 9.1 Recommended front-end stack

## 9.2 Performance budgets

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| LCP | <= 2.5s at 75th percentile on supported production pages. |
| INP | <= 200ms at 75th percentile. |
| CLS | <= 0.10. |
| Initial JS | Keep critical route payload lean; split 3D and non-critical motion bundles. |
| Images | Responsive, modern formats, explicit dimensions, lazy-loaded below the fold. |

## 9.3 WebGL and graceful degradation

- Detect WebGL capability and choose a fallback without showing an error state.
- Low-power / touch devices may receive reduced geometry, reduced particles, lower DPR or static media.
- All critical copy, navigation, CTAs and proof must exist in HTML and remain available if JavaScript or WebGL partially fails.
- Do not make SEO content dependent on canvas text.
## 9.4 Component architecture

- Build reusable primitives: Section, Container, Eyebrow, Heading, Button, Metric, LogoRail, CaseStudyPanel, Testimonial, SystemNode, WorkflowCard.
- Separate visual state from content data so the CMS can update copy and case studies without rewriting animation logic.
- Keep GSAP timelines scoped to components and clean them up on route change / unmount.
- Use feature flags / progressive activation for the heaviest 3D experiences during implementation.
**RESPONSIVE, ACCESSIBILITY AND SEO**

# 10. Quality Requirements

## 10.1 Responsive behavior

| Viewport | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Large desktop >= 1440px | Full spatial composition, 12-column layout, signature 3D, generous editorial scale. |
| Desktop 1024-1439px | Same narrative with reduced spacing, fewer simultaneous decorative elements. |
| Tablet 768-1023px | Simplified 3D, reduced pinned behavior, touch-first interactions, 8-column / fluid layout. |
| Mobile < 768px | Standard vertical flow, minimal / fallback 3D, no hover dependency, no forced horizontal scroll. |

## 10.2 Accessibility

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA.
- All navigation, interactive nodes, carousels, tabs and controls must support keyboard operation.
- Visible focus states must use high-contrast brand-consistent styling.
- Color cannot be the only indication of state or meaning.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Replace scroll-linked motion with direct content states where appropriate.
- Maintain sufficient text contrast on gradients and image / 3D backgrounds.
- Provide descriptive alt text for meaningful imagery and empty alt text for purely decorative media.
- Use semantic headings in order and meaningful link / button labels.
## 10.3 SEO and content discoverability

- Critical content must render in semantic HTML, not only inside canvas or animation.
- Maintain one clear H1 per page and a logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
- Retain or redirect important existing URLs to prevent equity loss during migration.
- Create unique metadata and structured content for Solutions, Growth Pods, AI, Work and Insights pages.
- Use structured data where appropriate for organization, articles, breadcrumbs and case-study-like content.
- Do not trade indexable copy for animation-only explanations.

**AI AGENT EXECUTION**

# 12. Instructions for the Design / Engineering AI Agent

> **Operating rule**
> Do not jump directly into coding the full homepage. Build and validate the system in phases. The signature hero and the disconnected-to-connected system interaction should be prototyped first because they establish the entire visual language.

## 12.1 Required execution order

1. Audit the current site content and map every important existing page / service to the proposed outcome-led architecture. Do not delete useful content merely because it is not on the homepage.
1. Create low-fidelity homepage wireframes for desktop and mobile using the 10-section narrative in this PRD.
1. Create the design tokens and reusable UI primitives before styling every section independently.
1. Prototype the hero Growth Core and the Disconnected -> Connected interaction in isolation. Validate performance and fallbacks.
1. Build the homepage shell with correct content hierarchy and responsive behavior before adding heavy motion.
1. Add motion progressively: basic reveals -> system interactions -> 3D -> polish.
1. Implement analytics, accessibility and reduced-motion behavior before final polish.
1. Run responsive, browser, performance and interaction QA before declaring the page complete.

## 12.3 AI agent guardrails

- Never add sections, copy claims, statistics or client results without source approval.
- Never copy a reference site section one-to-one. Translate principles into an original GN composition.
- Do not use placeholder lorem ipsum in final UI. Keep placeholders clearly marked during wireframing only.
- Do not create heavy scroll-jacking or trap the user inside pinned scenes.
- Do not rely on hover for information required on mobile or keyboard navigation.
- Do not introduce a new visual palette outside the approved GN colors unless explicitly approved.
- Do not make green and blue equally dominant across every section. Blue is the primary digital atmosphere; green is selective.
- Do not use more than 1-2 production WebGL signature experiences without an explicit performance review.
- Do not start with detailed 3D polish before the page hierarchy, content and conversion paths are correct.
